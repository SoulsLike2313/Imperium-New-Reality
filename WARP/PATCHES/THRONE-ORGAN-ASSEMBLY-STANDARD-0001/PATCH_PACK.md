## Purpose

Define what it means to raise an organ into real Core service.

This patch does not assemble organs. It creates the standard that future patches must satisfy.

## Owner law

An organ is assembled only if it has:

1. personal launcher/TUI command surface;
2. personal tools, scripts, functions, documents, and workflows;
3. personal validators for work-flow and organ-integrity;
4. Custodes validators against the organ;
5. Throne validators against the organ;
6. red-team and blue-team layers.
