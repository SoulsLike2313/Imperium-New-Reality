# THRONE CALLING V0.1

The Throne is called to make the Imperium measurable, regulatable, cleanable, and eventually enforceable.

It must turn vague ambition into:

- matrices;
- schemas;
- validators;
- receipts;
- reports;
- dashboards;
- gap maps;
- readiness scores;
- next-area recommendations.

The Throne must show the Owner what is missing and why.

It must not hide gaps.

It must not fake green.
