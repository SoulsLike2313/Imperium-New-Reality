## Decision

Administratum is to be built last among the Great Nine organs.

## Reason

Administratum should not become a registrar of emptiness.

Its proper function depends on the other organs first producing their own truthful self-description, composition, duties, tools, validators, receipts, reports, histories, dependencies, ownership maps, core/sub-core declarations and maturity state.

Administratum becomes strongest when it receives living reports from already-formed organs and turns them into registry, census, ownership, provenance, ledger, history and accountability systems.

Therefore Administratum is not the correct next primary organ after Astronomicon. It is a late-stage consolidation organ.

## Practical rule

Administratum may still receive small supporting documents, schemas or hooks when required by other patches.

But it must not be selected as the next primary Great Nine formation target until the other Great Nine organs have enough self-description and evidence to report into it.

## Throne boundary

This decision does not claim:

- Great Nine assembled;
- Administratum assembled;
- Throne complete;
- Core v1 ready;
- registry completeness;
- global organ ownership complete.

It only fixes Owner sequencing intent.

## Reference sequence law

```text
1. Build organs that unlock capability, evidence and validation.
2. Let each organ self-describe through the six maturity gates.
3. Let each organ produce receipts, reports, validators and history.
4. Build Administratum last as registrar/ledger over living organ reports.
```

## Current implication

The next primary organ should be selected by mathematical value, excluding Administratum from primary selection until late-stage Great Nine consolidation.
