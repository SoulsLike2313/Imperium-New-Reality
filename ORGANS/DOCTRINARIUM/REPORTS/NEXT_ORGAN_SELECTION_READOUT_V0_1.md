## Owner intent

Administratum is deferred to the last Great Nine consolidation stage.

Reason: Administratum should receive truthful reports from formed organs and turn them into registry, census, ownership, provenance, ledger and history. It should not become a registrar of incomplete organs.

## Why Mechanicus

Mechanicus has the highest practical Core v1 unlock value right now because it controls the boring reality that makes every other organ honest:

- environment;
- build;
- dependencies;
- tool versions;
- command execution;
- runtime proof;
- app proof;
- local machine assumptions;
- validator execution reliability;
- repair lanes.

If Mechanicus is weak, every later organ can accidentally stand on unstable ground.

## Why not Administratum now

Administratum raw value is high, but it depends on other organs reporting their composition, history, validators, receipts, reports and ownership maps.

Building it too early risks a beautiful registry over incomplete truth.

## Why not Doctrinarium as primary next

Doctrinarium is very valuable and should remain active as support. But it already received the six-gate canon. Its next value is school/law/schema expansion, while Mechanicus unlocks execution reliability and Core v1 substrate.
