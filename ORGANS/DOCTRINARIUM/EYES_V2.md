## 0. Premise — The Law of Eyes, restated

The eye is not an organ. It is the projection of a verified state onto a surface.
Beauty is honest signal. The eye must transmit what the kernel actually contains —
nothing more, nothing less. Decorative eyes are forbidden because they pretend.

This codebook compresses the owner's truth-feel into operational constraints
that any agent producing imagery for the empire (Boris avatars, sentinel badges,
agent icons, doctrinarium frontispieces, throne marks) must satisfy.

---

## 1. TONAL_FIELD — the ground

```
primary_hue    : 240..290 deg   (deep blue → violet → magenta-violet)
mean_value     : <= 60          (out of 255)
mean_saturation: 100..180
```

The ground is dark. Always. No daylight. No clean white field.
The empire emerges FROM dark, never floats IN light.

---

## 2. ACCENT_DISCIPLINE — the mark

```
accent_color     : yellow, hue 50..70, sat > 120, val > 180
accent_area      : 5..10% of frame
accent_role      : MARK, not fill. Stroke, glyph, edge highlight, point of contact.
competing_accents: 1   (two strong accents = noise)
```

The yellow is NOT in the references. It does not come from outside.
It is the empire's signature laid on top of the field. The references are mood;
the yellow is mark.

---

## 3. COMPOSITION_CLASS — the body

```
default       : FIGURE_IN_FIELD
subject_area  : 5..25% of frame
forbidden     : symmetric centered face filling > 40% of frame
gaze_default  : A_INWARD   (see §5)
```

The eye does not stare at the viewer.
It is present in the field but does not address the viewer.

---

## 4. COSMIC_VOLUME — the depth

```
required      : atmospheric depth, multi-plane composition, sense of vastness
forbidden     : flat decorative composition, foreground-only stickers,
                lookup-table-like color washes without depth cues
depth_signals : at least one of {
                  fog/haze layer,
                  scale contrast,
                  foreground-silhouette + far-receding plane,
                  atmospheric perspective
                }
```

Cosmic volume is the difference between a sticker and a window.
The empire's surfaces are windows.

---

## 5. GAZE_AXIS — the look

```
A_INWARD  (default) : pupil unseen / averted / lidded / downcast.
                      The eye looks INTO the eye, not OUT of it.
B_THROUGH (rare)    : direct contact with viewer through verified state.
                      Reserved for declarative moments
                      (e.g. emperor-seal activation, throne certifications).
forbidden           : open-frontal-smile, posed studio gaze,
                      decorative iris detail without purpose.
```

---

## 6. TPS_SPIRIT — Trash Polka as posture, not as syntax

Trash Polka is borrowed NOT as a literal collage technique
(no requirement of torn edges, no requirement of overlaid typography),
but as POSTURE:

- **brashness**  — the empire shows up loud, not polite
- **free form**  — composition is allowed to break frame, defy symmetry
- **free being** — the subject behaves like itself, not like a portrait subject

> Inherit the attitude. Drop the brushstrokes.

---

## 7. FORBIDDEN — explicit no

```
7.1  bright daylight fields (val_mean > 100 across the frame)
7.2  warm gold/amber dominance (warm_cool > +10)
7.3  flat color washes without depth (violates §4)
7.4  two competing accents > 5% each
7.5  symmetric centered frontal portrait (#17-class)
7.6  decorative typographic overlay
7.7  emoji / stock iconography
7.8  any color outside the validated palette without explicit doctrine update
```
