## Mission

The Throne is the Crown Organ that holds the form of the Imperium.

It does not replace the Great Nine. It surrounds, measures, regulates, and protects them.

The Great Nine are not a complete core without the Throne. The Throne cannot claim core status without the Great Nine.

## Core duty

The Throne validates the difference between:

```text
TARGET V1 FORM
CURRENT REALITY
```

A gap is not automatically a failure.

Failure begins when the gap cannot be measured, when an organ lies about its state, or when a mutation tries to enter Reality without proof.

## Relationship to the Great Nine

The Throne will validate and regulate organ actions through matrices, validators, receipts, and reports.

Each organ remains responsible for its own domain.

The Throne checks whether organ action is legal relative to the whole Imperium form.
