# THRONE FUNCTIONS V0.1

## Declared functions

1. Hold the target-v1 form of the Imperium.
2. Compare target-v1 form against current reality.
3. Validate the existence and integrity of the Throne itself.
4. Validate and regulate organ actions through future organ-zone validators.
5. Read population/census data and compute readiness/gap reports.
6. Hold validation modes: MEASURE_ONLY, WARN, WARP_ONLY, BLOCK_LAND, BLOCK_CYCLE.
7. Preserve Owner-only override policy.
8. Preserve visual freeze and allow only Eyes/data export, not visual refit.
9. Emit receipts and reports for every Throne validation.
10. Provide future TUI/dashboard/Eyes data surfaces for state visibility.

## What the Throne does not do yet

1. It does not enforce BLOCK.
2. It does not validate Astronomicon in detail yet.
3. It does not validate every organ yet.
4. It does not claim Imperium v1 readiness.
5. It does not mutate Eyes/Graph Viewer visuals.
