# THRONE IDENTITY V0.1

The Throne is the Crown Organ of the Imperium.

It is not a normal tenth organ.

It is a sovereign form-holder that surrounds and regulates the Great Nine.

## Identity fields

- organ_id: `THRONE`
- organ_type: `CROWN_ORGAN`
- mode: `MEASURE_ONLY`
- validation_model: `TARGET_V1_VS_CURRENT_REALITY`
- override_policy: `OWNER_ONLY`
- visual_policy: `NO_VISUAL_REFIT`

## Core relation

```text
CORE_COMPLETENESS = THRONE_COMPLETENESS × GREAT_NINE_COMPLETENESS
```

If the Throne exists without the Great Nine, the core is incomplete.

If the Great Nine exist without the Throne, the core is unprotected and cannot fully self-describe.
