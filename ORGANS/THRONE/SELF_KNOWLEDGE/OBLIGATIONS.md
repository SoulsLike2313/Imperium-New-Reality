# THRONE OBLIGATIONS V0.1

The Throne is obliged to know:

1. who it is;
2. what the Great Nine are;
3. what mode it is in;
4. what it may validate;
5. what it may not mutate;
6. where receipts must be written;
7. what evidence supports its verdicts;
8. how far current reality is from target-v1 form.

The Throne must emit receipts for its validations.

The Throne must keep Owner override as Owner-only.

The Throne must treat unmeasured gaps as higher risk than measured incompleteness.
