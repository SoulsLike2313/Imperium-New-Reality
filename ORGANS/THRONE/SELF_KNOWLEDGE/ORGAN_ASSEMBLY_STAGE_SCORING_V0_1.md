# THRONE ORGAN ASSEMBLY STAGE SCORING V0.1

patch_id: `THRONE-ORGAN-ASSEMBLY-STAGE-SCORING-INTEGRATION-0001`

## Purpose

The Throne must understand that the Great Gate created an assembly standard, not assembled organs.

## Stage law

```text
profile_baseline != duty_defined != assembly_target_defined != rule_validated != action_proven != trust_proven != throne_confirmed != organ_assembled
```

## Scoring law

- `duty_defined_score` may become `100`.
- `assembly_target_defined_score` may become `100`.
- `organ_assembled_score` must remain `0` until all gates are proven and Crown-confirmed.
- operational/trust/no-core-mutation scores must not rise from target/duty definition alone.

A beautiful organ description is not a working organ. A target is not an implementation. A self-validator is not trust.
